Privacy

What this site keeps

  • Your email address, to send you links to log in and, if this site is set up to, your game password. It is never shown to other players.
  • Your game account's name, to know which account is yours.
  • For a day or two, a note of each email sent: when, from which network address, and a scrambled form of the address it went to, so that the site can limit how many it sends.
  • A cookie that keeps you logged in, for two weeks or until you log out.

What the game server keeps

  • Your account name, your callsign, a hash of your password (never the password itself) and your progress.
  • Your callsign is shown to other players and on this site's status page.
  • Games, and the chat in them, may be recorded, as the server's operator has set it up.

Deleting

When you are logged in, your account page lets you delete your email address from this site, and your game account from the game server as well.
